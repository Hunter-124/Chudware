# Code of Conduct

Chudware adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies to everyone participating in the project's issues, pull
requests, discussions, and other community spaces, and to anyone representing the project
in public.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, welcome newcomers, and focus
criticism on ideas and code rather than people. Harassment, discriminatory language,
personal attacks, and publishing others' private information are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub: use
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/Hunter-124/Chudware/security/advisories/new)
for anything sensitive, or contact the maintainer through their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/Hunter-124). Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove,
edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate them.
Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
correction, warning, temporary ban, and permanent ban, depending on severity.
