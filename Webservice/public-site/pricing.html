Pricing

You are paying for storage and copilot use, not for features.

Every tier is the same application, running in your browser. What changes is how much project storage you keep on our side, how many copilot tokens are included, and whether you get shared PLM projects and the desktop build.

What the price is actually made of

Chudware is not cheap to run, and pretending otherwise would just produce a pricing page that has to change later. These are the three things that cost money.

  • Copilot tokens

    The AI copilot calls a language model, and that is billed per token upstream. It is by far the largest variable cost. Included allowances differ by tier; heavy use is metered instead of throttled silently.

  • Persistent storage

    Your projects and their vault history are real disk on our side that has to be kept, replicated and backed up. Storage is the main thing separating the tiers.

  • Engine compute

    Modelling kernel work — booleans, meshing, simulation — runs on our server, not in your browser. Sustained heavy jobs cost real CPU time and are accounted for per plan.
