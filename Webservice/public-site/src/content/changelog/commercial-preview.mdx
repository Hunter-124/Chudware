## Highlights

- Desktop and service diagnostics report the product version.
- Accounts support sign-in, email verification, and subscription management.
- Windows and Linux desktop packages are built on self-hosted runners.
- Searchable guides cover installation, modeling, assemblies, CAM, and AI setup.

## Browser and native access

Create an account to use the browser workspace. The [download page](/download/) lists available native builds for Windows, Linux, and macOS.

## September 17 — website and AI setup

- The homepage puts product screenshots and engineering workflows first.
- The gallery pairs requests and results with full-size screenshots. The modeling example includes a recorded copilot response; CAM and simulation examples are labeled as scripted scenarios.
- Product images retain their original proportions on desktop and mobile.
- AI setup uses your own supported provider account or API key. Credit-pack purchasing is unavailable; existing subscription billing and account records are unchanged.
- Pricing points to the current checkout rate, and download controls reflect published artifacts.
