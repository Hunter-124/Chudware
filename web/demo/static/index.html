Before you start

  • Your files persist Your workspace is saved on the server and the same browser reopens it next time. There is no total time limit; after 15 minutes without activity the workspace goes to sleep (its container stops) and resuming starts it again. Download a copy of anything important anyway.
  • Running limits Up to 3 people at a time, and each saved workspace is capped at 1 GiB. A workspace over the cap is put to sleep, not deleted.
  • AI models The AI assistant offers Claude Opus, Sonnet and Haiku 5.5 with a per-visitor allowance that can run out, plus free models with no demo limit (their provider may still ask you to wait a minute when it is busy). Modeling works without AI.
  • Privacy No account is needed. A cookie (kept for a year) identifies your workspace in this browser only: clearing cookies or using another browser starts a new, empty one, and the old one cannot be recovered. Your IP address, timestamps and per-session request counts are logged for abuse prevention; your files and prompts are not logged. Use Delete my saved files above to erase your workspace.

Experimental software: results may be wrong. Do not upload confidential or personal data.
