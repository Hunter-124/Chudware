Before you start

  • Nothing persists. Your workspace is deleted when the session ends. Download your work before then; a banner reminds you five minutes ahead.
  • Session limits. Up to 3 people at a time; a session ends after 45 minutes, or after 15 minutes without activity.
  • Free AI models only. The AI assistant uses free models on a small shared daily allowance, so it can run out. Modeling works without it.
  • Privacy. No account is needed. A cookie identifies your session in this browser only. Your IP address, timestamps and per-session request counts are logged for abuse prevention; your files and prompts are not logged and are deleted with the session.
  • Experimental software: results may be wrong. Do not upload confidential or personal data.
