## Context

HUNTER (2026-07-03): the Feature Manager (left glass column) and Copilot (right
glass column) currently collapse **horizontally** (slide/shrink sideways, e.g. the
« button at the top of the FM). Change both to collapse **vertically** — the panel
rolls up toward the top of the viewport, leaving its header strip (with the
collapse button) in place. The collapse buttons themselves STAY at the top where
they are now; only the collapse direction/animation and the collapsed footprint
change.

Fits the locked overlay design intent (pinned glass columns over the full-width
viewport — gui-overlay-design-decisions): a vertically-collapsed column frees the
viewport underneath it while keeping the column's header pinned and discoverable.
