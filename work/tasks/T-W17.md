## Steps

   consumer-OAuth kinds), and a per-role assignment table covering EVERY role in

   sees what strongest/cheapest currently means). Hunter directive 2026-07-04:
   shipped assignments are initial defaults only; the GUI is the owner. Warn
   inline (never block) on risky choices (overseer on a small local model).
   Also: delegation approval policy, toggles (auto-coincident, preflight,
   advisory verbosity). Validation errors shown inline; writes go through
